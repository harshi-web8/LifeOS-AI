🚀 LifeOS AI

Your All-in-One AI-Powered Life Management Platform

LifeOS AI is an intelligent and user-friendly web application designed to simplify everyday life. It brings together education, health & wellness, productivity, personal management, and AI-powered assistance in one centralized platform.

Built using Google AI Studio and integrated with Firebase, LifeOS AI aims to provide users with a smarter, more organized, and efficient digital experience.

---

📌 About the Project

LifeOS AI is an AI-powered platform developed to support users in managing different aspects of their lives.

The platform focuses on making daily activities easier through smart digital tools, personalized assistance, and cloud-based data management.

It is designed for students, individuals, and users who want to improve their productivity, organize their routines, and manage their daily activities efficiently.
